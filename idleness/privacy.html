Idleness

Privacy

All records remain on this device.

Updated September 23, 2026

Who this covers

This policy describes the Idleness apps for iOS and Android, published by Twepx. Idleness does not have accounts, and it does not run its own servers for gameplay.

What stays on your device

Progress, statistics, achievements, settings, and unlocked materials are stored only on the device. That includes sound, haptics, and reduced-motion preferences, session counters, and a local cache of which licenses this install believes it owns.

We cannot see that record. Reset Local Progress in the Service Panel deletes it. Uninstalling the app deletes it with the app.

Motion

Some machine procedures read motion sensors, such as tilt or shake, while you are playing. Those samples are used as input in the moment. They are not saved as a movement history and they are not sent to us.

Purchases

Licenses are optional one-time purchases. Apple or Google processes the payment. Idleness does not receive your card number, billing address, or full payment details.

To check and restore licenses, the app uses RevenueCat. When a network connection is available, RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app user id created on the device, purchase receipts, entitlement status, and technical data needed to complete the request, including platform, app version, and the IP address of the request.

That information is used to know which licenses you own and to restore them. It is not used for advertising. If the store or RevenueCat cannot be reached, free play continues.

RevenueCat’s own policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • An account or login
  • Location
  • Photos, camera, or microphone audio
  • Advertising identifiers
  • A gameplay profile that we can read

Idleness does not include third-party analytics or advertising. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Store consoles

Apple and Google may show us aggregated install, sales, crash, and performance information in App Store Connect and the Play Console. That reporting is controlled by Apple and Google. It is not a copy of your on-device Operator Record.

How long information is kept

On-device records stay until you reset progress or delete the app. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies so a license can be restored later. We do not keep a separate purchase database.

Your choices

  • Reset local progress from the Service Panel
  • Uninstall the app
  • Restore purchases from the Service Panel or the License screen
  • Manage or request a refund for a purchase through Apple or Google

To ask for deletion of the anonymous RevenueCat purchase record for an install, contact us through the support address on the Idleness store listing and include the store order id. Apple and Google keep their own transaction records, and we cannot delete those.

Children

Idleness is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Where purchase data is processed

Purchase checks may be processed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat in the United States and in other countries where those companies operate.

Changes

We will change the date on this policy when it changes. The current date is September 23, 2026. The updated policy will be available in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support address published with Idleness on the App Store or Google Play. The app is published by Twepx.
